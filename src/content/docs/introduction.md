---
title: What is BountyPort?
---

BountyPort is a decentralized bounty platform for open-source software. Our mission is to provide a transparent and efficient way for maintainers to fund specific tasks and for contributors to earn rewards for their work.

## Core Features (MVP)
- **Bounty Creation**: Maintainers can create bounties with a specified reward.
- **Bounty Funding**: Maintainers can fund bounties with cryptocurrency.
- **Work Submission**: Contributors can submit their work for a specific bounty.
- **Bounty Approval**: Maintainers can approve submissions and release funds.
- **Bounty Payout**: Funds are automatically released to the contributor upon approval.

## Why BountyPort?
- **Trustless**: Payments are handled by smart contracts, eliminating the need for intermediaries.
- **Transparent**: All bounty activities and fund distributions are recorded on the blockchain.
- **Efficient**: Automated workflows reduce administrative overhead for maintainers.

BountyPort is currently in an **MVP** stage, focusing on the core bounty lifecycle.
